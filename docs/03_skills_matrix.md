## Quantum Technologies Application Angle

For a quantum technologies master's application, the most relevant transferable
strengths are:

- computational and mathematical maturity from a Computer Science background;
- C/C++ and systems programming experience;
- research software development in applied technical domains;
- comfort with simulation, computer graphics, image processing, and hardware
  interfaces;
- experience with augmented reality, human-computer interaction, and
  accessibility-oriented applied research;
- experience working in academic research teams and international environments.

Avoid claiming direct quantum technology experience unless separate evidence is
added. The current dossier supports preparation for the field through computing,
software engineering, research collaboration, and technical adaptability.
