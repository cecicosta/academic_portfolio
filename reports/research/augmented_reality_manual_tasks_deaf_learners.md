# Augmented Reality for Remote Teaching of Manual Tasks

Status: draft  
Last updated: 2026-07-04

## Summary

Cecilia Lis Costa worked in 2017 as a Software Developer and Research Assistant
on a research project focused on augmented reality, wearable devices, and remote
instruction of manual tasks for deaf and hard-of-hearing learners.

The project investigated accessible interaction methods for vocational and
manual-task education, combining software development, human-computer
interaction, accessibility, augmented reality, and experimental evaluation.

## Project Context

The work was connected to research at LEAD, the Laboratory for Distance Education
for People with Disabilities, and supported the master's dissertation of Thiago
A. C. de Araujo at the State University of Ceara.

The dissertation and article describe the broader research problem: improving
remote instruction of practical tasks through augmented reality learning objects
and wearable devices. The article PDF reports an experiment with 80 participants
using an augmented reality learning object with four forms of interaction.

## My Role and Contributions

I contributed as a Software Developer and Research Assistant. My work included
software implementation and research support for the augmented reality learning
object used in the study.

My responsibilities included:

- developing software components for the augmented reality application;
- supporting the design and refinement of the learning object used in the
  evaluation;
- contributing to research activities related to accessibility, augmented
  reality, human-computer interaction, and distance education;
- supporting user-study preparation and execution;
- assisting with analysis and interpretation of experimental results;
- contributing as a coauthor to the article on the interaction of deaf learners
  with an augmented reality application on wearable devices.
