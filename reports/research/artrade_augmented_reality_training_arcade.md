# ARTRADE - Augmented Reality Training Arcade

Status: draft  
Last updated: 2026-07-04

## Summary

Cecilia Lis Costa served as Lead Researcher and Software Developer for ARTRADE,
the Augmented Reality Training Arcade project, between 2018 and 2019.

The project was developed at LEAD, the Laboratory for Distance Education for
People with Disabilities, with Dell Technologies as an industry partner. It
extended previous augmented reality research for accessible learning into
industrial training and workforce qualification contexts.

## Project Context

ARTRADE applied augmented reality to industrial training scenarios, especially
assembly and manufacturing procedures. The project built on earlier work in
augmented reality, accessibility, wearable devices, and remote instruction of
manual tasks.

Because the project operated under industrial confidentiality agreements, the
current report avoids detailed technical claims that are not present in the local
source note or otherwise cleared for public use.

## My Role and Contributions

I led research and software development activities for the continuation and
technical evolution of the augmented reality training platform.

My responsibilities included:

- leading technical evolution from the previous research prototype;
- designing, developing, and implementing new software components;
- conducting applied research on augmented reality, human-computer interaction,
  computer vision, accessibility, and industrial training methods;
- evaluating and refining interaction techniques for usability and efficiency in
  industrial training scenarios;
- collaborating with multidisciplinary research and engineering teams;
- planning milestones and coordinating technical activities under project
  supervision;
- preparing technical documentation and supporting internal validation of
  research outcomes;
- preparing and presenting technical progress, prototypes, and research findings
  to Dell Technologies teams in Campinas, Brazil.
