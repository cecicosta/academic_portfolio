# Virtual Shooting Training System

Status: draft  
Last updated: 2026-07-04

## Summary

Cecilia Lis Costa contributed to the Virtual Shooting Training System, also known
as Projeto Tiro Virtual, a Federal University of Ceara research and development
project carried out around 2010-2013.

The project developed a virtual firearms training simulator combining interactive
video, 3D computer graphics, laser-based shot capture, adapted hardware, and
performance analysis.

## Project Context

Public FUNCAP and UFC references describe the system as a low-cost virtual
training platform for public security use. The FUNCAP article states that UFC
researchers had been developing the system since February 2010 and that the
project was requested by the Public Security Secretariat of the State of Ceara
and financially supported through FUNCAP.

The UFC article reports that the system used projection, real firearms adapted
without ammunition, laser marking, and performance reports. It also records that
the system was presented at EXPOSEC in Sao Paulo between May 8 and 10, 2012, and
identifies participating UFC researchers from UFC Virtual and the Department of
Computer Science.

## Technical Scope

The project involved:

- interactive video scenarios;
- 3D virtual environments;
- laser-based hit detection;
- adapted real firearms and optical tracking;
- real-time performance assessment;
- prototype testing and demonstrations;
- C++ and Linux development according to the FUNCAP public reference.

## My Role and Contributions

I worked on the project as an undergraduate software developer between 2010 and
2013. Working notes and the scanned recommendation letter identify me as the main
software developer during this period.

My responsibilities included:

- implementing and evolving simulator software;
- integrating software modules required for simulator operation;
- supporting prototype development and refinement;
- debugging, testing, and validating system behavior;
- participating in demonstrations and project presentations;
- collaborating with faculty researchers and other members of the development
  team.
