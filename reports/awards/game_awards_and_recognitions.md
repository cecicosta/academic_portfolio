# Supernova Game Projects, Awards, and Recognitions

Status: draft  
Last updated: 2026-07-04

## Summary

Current source notes and user-provided context identify Supernova project
context for Carcara - Asas da Justica, Vetor, and Heroes Rush Tactics. The
currently documented competitive funding selections and festival recognitions
are for Carcara - Asas da Justica and Vetor. Heroes Rush Tactics is also
recorded here because it was reportedly presented at the Brazilian Symposium on
Games and Digital Entertainment (SBGames) in 2012.

These projects should be grouped under Cecilia Lis Costa's reported role as
co-founder of Supernova, an independent game development team active during that
period. Until primary role evidence is added, the recognitions should be
described as Supernova project or team recognitions rather than individual
awards.

Tardigrade should not be included in this report. It remains a separate
master's-period academic publication.

## Recognitions

### Carcara - Asas da Justica

Recognition: selected for competitive federal funding  
Program: INOVApps Program  
Institution: Brazilian Ministry of Communications  
Year: 2014

The source note describes Carcara - Asas da Justica as an educational serious
game designed to teach Brazilian Consumer Protection Law through interactive
storytelling and gameplay. The project was competitively selected through the
INOVApps Program, a nationwide initiative supporting innovation in mobile
applications and digital games.

References listed in the source note:

- UOL: `https://www.uol.com.br/start/ultimas-noticias/2015/05/29/jogo-nacional-gratis-sobre-advogados-diverte-e-ensina-direito-do-consumidor.htm`
- Baguete: `https://www.baguete.com.br/noticias/apps-recebem-ate-r-100-mil-do-minicom`
- Supernova Games: `https://supernova.games/carcara-br/`
