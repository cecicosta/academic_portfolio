## Materials Physics

The KTH course context describes a course on classical and quantum mechanical
models of intrinsic properties of materials. Topics include:

- crystal structures and reciprocal space;
- fundamentals of quantum mechanics;
- free electrons in metals;
- electrons in periodic potentials;
- band structure;
- electronic structure methods;
- cohesive properties;
- atomistic theory of elasticity;
- lattice vibrations;
- semiconductors;
- magnetism.

The intended learning outcomes emphasize explaining physical concepts in crystal
structures, electronic structures, lattice vibrations, and magnetism, and
analyzing problems where an atomic-level approach is preferable.

## Computational Physics

The Malmo University course context describes an online introductory course
focused on numerical methods for modeling physical systems and implementation in
Python.

Course activities included assignments on:

- introduction to Python;
- programming with Python;
- interpolation and curve fitting;
- integrals and roots;
- differential equations;
- project planning;
- eigenvalue problems;
- Monte Carlo methods;
- a final project report.

The submitted project report was titled `Atomic Structure` and was handed in on
2026-05-22. The report discusses Schrodinger's equation, radial wave functions,
atomic structure, eigenvalue/eigenstate problems, and computational methods for
approximating atomic systems.
