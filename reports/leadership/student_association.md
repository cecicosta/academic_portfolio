## Institutional Context

The Computer Science Student Association represented undergraduate students in
dialogue with faculty, department leadership, and university administration. Its
activities included academic representation, integration of incoming students,
student communication, and organization of academic and scientific initiatives.

## My Role and Contributions

I served as a student representative and organizer. My work included
representing student concerns in academic settings, supporting communication
between students and faculty, and helping organize activities that strengthened
the undergraduate Computer Science community.

Key activities included:

- participation in student representation before faculty, the Department of
  Computer Science, and the Center of Sciences;
- support for Semana Zero, the orientation activity for incoming students;
- organization of academic and scientific activities for undergraduate students;
- contribution to the first organized student excursion to a scientific event,
  which became a model for later student participation.
