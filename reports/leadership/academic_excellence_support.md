# Academic Excellence Support Program

Status: draft  
Last updated: 2026-07-04

## Summary

Cecilia Lis Costa participated as a peer mentor in the Academic Excellence
Support Program for the Computer Science Undergraduate Program at the Federal
University of Ceara.

The program was connected to the Undergraduate Project Monitoring Program,
`Edital 18/2009`, and the working record identifies the official project title
as `Suporte Academico para Excelencia do Bacharelado em Computacao`.

## Program Context

The program aimed to support academic excellence and the integration of first-year
Computer Science students. It combined peer mentoring, faculty supervision,
regular communication with student groups, and activities intended to improve the
undergraduate experience.

Current working records identify:

- institution: Federal University of Ceara;
- coordinator: Prof. Creto Augusto Vidal;
- course: Computer Science undergraduate program, course code 65;
- start date: 2010-03-08;
- expected end date: 2010-12-31.

## My Role and Contributions

I participated as a student mentor, supporting incoming Computer Science students
as they adapted to university life and the undergraduate program.

My responsibilities included:

- attending regular meetings with faculty advisors;
- preparing or contributing to meeting records;
- acting as a communication link between assigned students, faculty advisors, and
  course coordination;
- representing the assigned student group when needed;
- collaborating with other mentors;
- supporting lectures, seminars, academic activities, and integration events;
- helping identify student needs and propose improvements to the undergraduate
  program.
