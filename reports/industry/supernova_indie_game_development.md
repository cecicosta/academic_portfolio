# Supernova Indie Game Development

Status: draft  
Last updated: 2026-07-04

## Summary

Cecilia Lis Costa reports having been a co-founder of Supernova, an independent
game development team active during the Carcara - Asas da Justica, Vetor, and
Heroes Rush Tactics projects.

Heroes Rush Tactics was reportedly presented at the Brazilian Symposium on Games
and Digital Entertainment (SBGames) in 2012.

This report is the parent context for the portfolio's indie game development
experience and for the documented Carcara/Vetor team or project recognitions. It
should not include Tardigrade, which is a separate master's-period academic
publication.

## Recognitions Connected to Supernova Projects

- Carcara - Asas da Justica was selected for competitive federal funding through
  the INOVApps Program of the Brazilian Ministry of Communications in 2014.
- Vetor was selected for competitive federal funding through the INOVApps
  Program of the Brazilian Ministry of Communications in 2015.
- Vetor won Best Educational Game Project at BIG Starter / Brazil's Independent
  Games Festival in 2016.
- Carcara - Asas da Justica was a finalist for Best Educational or Social Impact
  Game at Brazil's Independent Games Festival in 2016.
